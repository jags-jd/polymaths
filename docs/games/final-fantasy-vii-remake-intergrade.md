## Structure (from the mockup, 218 units)

Sections: Chapters 1–18, Episode INTERmission, Whole Game.

Each chapter has one full-width chapter card with grouped trackables (all `check`):

| Group | Icon | Totals |
| ----- | ---- | ------ |
| Progress (Cleared, Cleared on Hard) | tick | 18 × 2 |
| Side quests | tick | 26 (Ch 3: 6, Ch 8: 6, Ch 9: 5, Ch 14: 9) |
| Music discs | disc | 31 |
| Weapons | sword | 24 (+2 Yuffie) |
| Summon materia | orb | 5 in chapters (+1 Ramuh, +4 under Whole Game) |
| Bridal candidates (Ch 9) | dress | 9 |

…followed by boss cards (`gallery`, image slot, "Defeated"). Chapter 17–18 bosses are spoilers.

INTERmission: both chapters (Cleared, Hard), Yuffie's weapons, Fort Condor (7 opponents + grandmaster + Hard), 6 Happy Turtle flyers, Shinra Box Buster, Ramuh, bosses Gigantipede, Scarlet and the Crimson Mare, Nero (spoiler), Pride and Joy Mk 0.5, Weiss (spoiler).

Whole Game: 20 Battle Intel reports, other summons, mini-games, story extras, mastery feats, platinum.
